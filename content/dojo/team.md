Every class at East Wind is led by one of two chief instructors, backed by an instructor team trained in-house — two world champions among them.

Sensei Mike and Sensei Laura met at a karate seminar in 1992, founded Eastwind Martial Arts in 1995, and were married in 1999. Today they're the proud parents of four children, ages 23, 21, 19, and 16 — all four of whom have followed in their footsteps and train in martial arts. Over the years they've travelled the world together, training, teaching, and competing at tournaments, from local dōjōs to international competitions and, most importantly, to Okinawa, the birthplace of Karate-dō. For Mike and Laura, the journey is never about reaching a final destination — their goal is to keep learning, keep challenging themselves, grow as martial artists and teachers, and share that knowledge with their students.
