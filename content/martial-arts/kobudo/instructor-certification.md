The Okinawa Kobudo Dōshi Rensei-kai (OKDR) runs a six-level certification program leading to a shodan-level teaching credential. It's built for two kinds of students: school owners who want to offer a complete, internationally recognized Kobudo curriculum, and advanced students who want to teach, or simply go deeper into a complementary art.

All classes toward certification must be taught under a certified OKDR black belt. There are two pathways through the program — a green belt track and a black belt track — reflecting different levels of commitment rather than different curricula.

Advancement depends on more than attendance — time at your current level and demonstrated skill both factor in before you move up. Ask an instructor for current program details and fees.
