The Okinawa Kobudo Dōshi Rensei-kai (OKDR) runs a six-level certification program leading to a shodan-level teaching credential. It's built for two kinds of students: school owners who want to offer a complete, internationally recognized Kobudo curriculum, and advanced students who want to teach, or simply go deeper into a complementary art.

All classes toward certification must be taught under a certified OKDR black belt.

## How the levels build

| Level | Class minimum | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 12 classes | Bo fundamentals, stances, basic technique |
| 2 | 20 classes | Interactive bo practice, sai introduced |
| 3 | 30 classes | Sai kata, tonfa fundamentals |
| 4 | 30 classes | Advanced bo applications |
| 5 | 40 classes | Nunchaku basics, approaching shodan candidacy |
| Shodan | — | Demonstrated competency across all four weapons |

## Two tracks

- **Green belt program** — minimum 32 classes. Roughly $475 all-in, covering tuition, testing, the organization patch, and annual OKDR membership.
- **Black belt program** — minimum 132 classes, over roughly two years. Roughly $1,670 all-in, covering the same categories at the black-belt level.

Advancement depends on more than attendance — time at your current level and demonstrated skill both factor in before you move up.
