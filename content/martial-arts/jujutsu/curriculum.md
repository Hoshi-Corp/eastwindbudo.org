East Wind's jujutsu draws on three related traditions: affiliated federation schools, the Yudansha Jujutsu Federation (YJJF) curriculum, and Yamanaka-ha Shindō-ryū itself.

## The YJJF curriculum

A published syllabus, partly built on standards from the JuJitsu International Federation, using the familiar modern kyu/dan ranking system — set requirements at each belt level.

## The Yamanaka-ha Shindō-ryū structure

A traditional, non-competitive system, licensed the old way: through *menkyo*, not belts. Technique is organized into five progressive levels of transmission:

- **Shoden** — entry level
- **Chūden** — middle level
- **Jōden** — high level
- **Okuden** — hidden level
- **Kuden** — oral transmission of what's never written down

Technical rank (*dan-i*) runs up to eighth degree. Teaching licenses are separate from technical rank — earning one requires at least fifth-degree proficiency, and completed scrolls from shoden through jōden at minimum.
