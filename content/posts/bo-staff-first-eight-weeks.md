What to expect the first time you pick up a weapon.

Kobudo students usually start on the bo staff before moving to sai or nunchaku. The first few weeks are about grip, distance, and control — not speed. We'd rather you learn to stop a strike cleanly than swing fast and sloppy.

By week eight, most students are running basic strike-and-block combinations solo, and starting to pair up for controlled partner drills.
