A short reflection on etiquette, and why the small rituals matter.

Every class at East Wind opens and closes with a bow — to the room, to the instructor, and to the students beside you. New students sometimes ask if it means something religious. It doesn't. It's a habit of attention: a moment where everyone in the room agrees to take the next hour seriously, and to look out for each other on the floor.

That's it. No belt required to understand it, and no mystery once you've done it a few times.
